A cardio motivation app that builds a world.

Give your walking, running, cycling, or other cardio a visible reward. Cardia World turns recorded cardio into a growing 3D place you can explore, name, and share with friends.

See your consistency take shape.

A completed workout can feel abstract once the timer stops. In Cardia World, qualifying cardio adds to the same world over time: a clearing becomes a home, then neighborhoods, towns, cities, and eventually a planet. The world is a record of your effort, with residents, scenery, and places to discover as you progress.

That growth takes repeated activity. A city or planet represents accumulated progress, rather than a promise that one workout unlocks everything.

A simple loop for your existing routine.

  1. Choose your daily cardio goal. Pick a goal that fits the routine you already follow. At least 15 recorded cardio minutes in a day qualifies for construction progress.
  2. Record your activity. Log cardio manually, or allow Cardia World to read supported workouts from Apple Health on a compatible iPhone. Separate sessions on the same day contribute to the daily total.
  3. Explore what you built. Open World to see your settlement, then return to keep building through consistent activity.

A fitness game with room for your own style.

Start with Cozy Heritage, Modern Grove, or Future Bloom. Each has its own buildings and atmosphere. As your world grows, you can name the places you create and visit friends’ worlds after connecting in Friends.

The core world-building experience and Friends are free. Optional Cardia World Pro adds premium styles, customization, and Cardio Battles. You can keep using the free experience without subscribing.

Before your first build

Do I need Apple Health to use the app?

No. Manual cardio logging is available on iPhone and iPad. Apple Health is optional on a compatible iPhone and requires your permission.

Is this a workout plan?

Cardia World helps you record activity and see game progress. You choose your own activity; the app does not prescribe a training program.

Can walking count as cardio?

Yes. Record walking as cardio manually, or sync a supported walking workout through Apple Health. Read the walking guide for the difference between steps and recorded minutes.
