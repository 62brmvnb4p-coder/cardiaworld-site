A daily habit with somewhere to grow.

Start with a goal that fits your routine. Record at least 15 cardio minutes on a date to make it a qualifying day. Several logs on the same date still count as one day.

01

Cardio builds. Steps bring life.

Walking, running, cycling and other recorded cardio help construction. Additional minutes contribute build points and small structures. Daily steps support vitality, happiness, greenery and weather.

Sync supported Apple Health workouts on a compatible iPhone or log manually. Apple Watch workouts can count when they are saved in Apple Health.

02

Your week, with room to rest

Choose planned cardio days in your weekly routine. Other days become protected rest days from the day you save. Set reminders for the days and times that work for you.

When you return after an absence, cardio helps restore your world while preserving earned progress.

03

Six styles, a personal world

Three free styles—Cozy Heritage, Modern Grove and Future Bloom—give you different ways to begin. Pro adds Neo City, Solar Garden and Zen Valley.

Your world follows local day and night, with evening lights and moving details. Explore homes and headquarters, and start earning naming rewards from cardio day three.

04

A private world journal

Save a real captured view from World, then revisit it in Progress → World journal. Keep your latest 36 moments and compare how far you have come.

In the current App Store app, journal images stay on this device, separate from world-progress backup. Share an image only when you choose.

05

See what your activity earned

Check cardio days, lifetime minutes, the next reward and your activity history. Open How did this count? for an explanation of newly credited activity.

Construction recaps highlight what you earned. You can adjust automatic recaps in Settings and review the change when you are ready.

06

Explore at your pace

Rotate, zoom and tap buildings for a closer look. Develop your style’s headquarters from a foundation into a more complex landmark.

Use labeled camera controls, Battery-saving world and optional guidance. Send feedback from Settings, with device details and a world image included only if you choose them.

Make your movement part of a story

Friends to visit. Mysteries to follow.

Friends, messages and gifts

Connect by username or a friend invitation, visit each other’s worlds, and explore named places and displayed gifts. Friends can see shared cardio-day and lifetime-minute totals.

Message friends with 14-day conversation history. Once both friends reach three cardio days, exchange gifts using Cardia dollars earned through activity.

Mail, Mystery and Research

A hint arrives on cardio day two. From day three, follow a 24-chapter mystery written for your world style and choose your replies.

From cardio day five, use Research to discover special structures and gifts. A separate Gift Shop makes earned keepsakes easy to find and send.

Your mayor writes to you

Hear from your world’s mayor when missed planned cardio days affect the town and when your return begins its recovery. Letters arrive inside the app and remain available in the correspondence archive.

Protected rest days do not count as missed days. Reading a letter does not change your activity or earned progress.

Monthly Pro collections

Eight keepsakes. New goals throughout the month.

Qualifying cardio days during the month unlock targets at days 1, 3, 5, 8, 12, 16, 20 and 24. Dates do not need to be consecutive. Preview every piece, collect eligible pieces with Pro, and display three owned favorites at once. Earned pieces remain yours if your subscription ends.

Coming in the next app update

Your world’s next chapter.

These features are built and currently in private TestFlight testing. They are not yet part of the public App Store version. This preview describes what is on the way.

Six seconds of your world in motion

Create a short rotating video automatically from a saved journal view. The footer shows the total cardio minutes saved with that moment.

Creating the first video earns a Showcase Pavilion. Creations in later weeks can add banners, up to three appearances. Posting to Stories is always optional.

One-link invitations, a shared garden

Send one invitation link. A new friend opens it and taps Accept invitation, with no code to type.

When they accept within their account’s first seven days and complete three different qualifying cardio days after acceptance, you both earn a Friendship Garden. Connecting as friends remains a separate choice.

Choose your next small building

Pick a greenhouse, cozy café or windmill. Starting when you first open the update, every three qualifying cardio days earns the next selected small build.

These extras sit alongside your main town’s progression. Every qualifying cardio day retains construction credit, including days spent rebuilding.

Meet your residents

Named neighbors react to your progress and your world’s condition. Help Pip choose a flower or vegetable garden for the terrace.

Your choices give the place your cardio builds a little more personality.

Build the Together Fountain

Opt into a weekly community effort toward 30 combined qualifying cardio days. Join first, then complete three different qualifying days of your own to earn the Community Fountain when the group finishes.

The project shows combined day totals. Participation is optional.

Pro saving · Free preview

Your terrace, your design

Arrange earned small buildings, rewards, gardens and paths across nine terrace plots. Rotate pieces and save three named layouts with Pro.

Open saved layouts in a full-screen preview. Save and Apply shows the resulting layout immediately. Your active saved layout remains after Pro ends.

Your world on the Home Screen

Add a small or medium widget on a supported iPhone or iPad. See a saved world image, your cardio-minute total and the next reward at a glance.

Enable it in the app, then add it with your device’s Home Screen widget controls.

Take your journal to a new device

Turn on optional private journal backup to keep your latest 36 captures and saved rotating views with your account.

Backup starts off. Restore your moments on another device, or turn backup off and delete the cloud journal. This is separate from ordinary world-progress backup.

Richer worlds, smoother exploration

More detailed materials, lighting and atmosphere across all six styles bring the landscape to life. Camera transitions keep you in the world as you move between places.

Explore your own world and your friends’ worlds through the same continuous view.

Availability will be updated here after the new app version is released. Existing image-only journal captures remain available; they do not invent a past 3D view or historical minute total.
