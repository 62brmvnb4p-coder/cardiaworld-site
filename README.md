## Download attribution

App Store download buttons use Apple's campaign links with provider token `129494535`, generated in App Store Connect. The fallback campaign is `website`, including when JavaScript is disabled. Canonical URLs and structured data retain the ordinary App Store URL.

For social traffic, use `https://cardiaworld.com/?from=tiktok` (or `facebook` / `instagram`). Add `&utm_medium=paid_social` for paid traffic. Standard `utm_source` values for those networks also work. The site carries the channel across its public marketing pages and sets the App Store campaign to `tiktok_organic`, `facebook_organic`, `instagram_organic`, or the corresponding `_paid` campaign. Other source values fall back to `website`. No cookies, local storage, visitor identifiers, or third-party analytics scripts are used.

These campaign labels measure future attributed App Store activity; button clicks are not install counts. Apple may withhold small campaign cohorts and reports results after a delay. Safari's Smart App Banner is a separate path and is not included in this button attribution setup.

## Discovery

Public discovery pages have unique titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, social metadata, and breadcrumb structured data. The homepage links to all guides; the sitemap lists the six canonical public pages. The app video is unchanged and has its own watch page. Search Console verification and indexing requests must be completed in the owner’s approved Google account; a published sitemap alone does not prove submission or indexing.
