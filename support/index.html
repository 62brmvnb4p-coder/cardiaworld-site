Frequently asked questions

Quick answers

What counts as a qualifying day?

Record at least 15 minutes of cardio in one day. Your selected goal may be 15, 20, 30, or 40 minutes, but 15 minutes is the minimum needed to advance construction.

Can I enter more than one workout in a day?

Yes. Separate sessions recorded on the same day are combined toward your daily total.

Where is my progress stored?

Version 1 stores your cardio entries, settings, and world progress locally on your device. It does not require an account or use cloud storage.

Does Cardia World connect to Apple Health?

Not in Version 1. Cardio is entered manually. Apple Health integration may be considered for a future update, with updated permissions and privacy information.

How do I change my daily goal?

Open Settings in the app and choose 15, 20, 30, or 40 minutes as your daily target.

How do I erase my information?

Open Settings and select Erase all local data. This resets locally stored progress and cannot be undone.

I found a problem. What should I send?

Email us with the screen you were viewing, what you expected to happen, what happened instead, your device model, and your system version. A screenshot is helpful when it does not reveal private information.

Is Cardia World medical or fitness advice?

No. Cardia World is a motivational world-building game and not a medical device. It does not diagnose, monitor, or treat health conditions.
