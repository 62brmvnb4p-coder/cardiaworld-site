Frequently asked questions

Quick answers

How do I reset my password?

Choose Forgot password? on the app’s sign-in screen, or request a reset email here. Open the newest email from noreply@auth.cardiaworld.com, choose a new password, then return to the app and sign in. Check Spam or Junk if the email does not appear.

My email already has an account. Should I sign up again?

Choose Sign in with that email. If you don’t remember the password, reset it using the link above. Your original account and progress stay with you.

What counts as a qualifying day?

Record at least 15 minutes of cardio in one day. You can choose a goal from 15 to 480 minutes, including a custom whole-number goal. Fifteen minutes is the minimum needed to advance construction.

Can I enter more than one workout in a day?

Yes. Separate sessions recorded on the same day are combined toward your daily total.

Where is my progress stored?

Your device stores activity, settings, and world progress. When you sign in, a private account backup can restore your world on another device. Friends only see the world details permitted by your privacy choices.

Does Cardia World connect to Apple Health?

Yes, if you grant permission on a compatible iPhone. Steps and recorded workouts can update your world. You can also log cardio manually.

How do I change my daily goal?

Open Settings in the app and choose a preset or enter a custom daily goal from 15 to 480 minutes.

How do I erase my information?

Open Settings and select Erase all local data to reset this device’s world and its signed-in account backup. To delete the account and associated server records, use Delete my account in Friends. These are separate actions.

I found a problem. What should I send?

Email us with the screen you were viewing, what you expected to happen, what happened instead, your device model, and your system version. A screenshot is helpful when it does not reveal private information.

Is Cardia World medical or fitness advice?

No. Cardia World is a motivational world-building game and not a medical device. It does not diagnose, monitor, or treat health conditions.
