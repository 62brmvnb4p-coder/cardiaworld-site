Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Cardia World uses fitness activity to build your in-game world and support features you choose. This policy describes what we collect, whom we share it with, and how you can exercise your privacy rights.

What we collect and why

  • When you connect activity sync, we read steps and workout information from Apple Health with your permission. The app may also count steps through Motion & Fitness on your iPhone.
  • We record cardio minutes you enter in the app and activity goals you set. We use daily step and cardio totals, workout type, start and end times, duration, and source to update your activity history, world, rewards, and goals.
  • We derive game stats such as qualifying cardio days, streaks, challenge scores, and world conditions from your activity. We use these to run features such as Battles and missions when you participate.
  • We use account identifiers to associate activity with your account. Private cloud backups hold logged and imported activity so you can restore progress. Original Apple Health workout identifiers stay on your device.

Who can receive the data

Cloud hosting and account service providers, including Supabase, process and store activity so Cardia World can run and restore your account. Providers that deliver game notifications or email can process the content of notices you receive, which may include an activity-based game summary or world view.

Other players can see selected totals, game scores, or world information when your privacy settings allow it or you join a Battle or mission. The public leaderboard can show your username and activity-derived world progress when you are visible. Other players do not receive your original workout records from these features. When you export a snapshot or video, you choose where to send it. We may disclose information when required by law. We do not sell consumer health data or use it to target ads.

Manage sync and sharing

Revoke Health and Motion & Fitness access in iPhone Settings to stop future reads on that device, or use Stop activity sync in Settings → Health sync where available. To limit what other people see, change Social privacy in Friends and hide your rank in Global leaderboard. Stopping sync leaves past activity in your account. You can request deletion separately.

Request access, withdrawal, or deletion

Email support@cardiaworld.com to confirm whether we collect or share your health data, ask for a copy and a list of recipients with contact details, withdraw consent for future collection or sharing, or request deletion. Tell us the account username or email and the request you want to make. Keep passwords and health records out of email. We may verify your identity before fulfilling the request.

If we decline a request, you can appeal by emailing support@cardiaworld.com with the subject “Consumer Health Data Appeal” and referencing our decision. Washington consumers can contact the Washington Attorney General if an appeal is denied.

For details about other types of data, see the Cardia World Privacy Policy.
