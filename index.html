Is Cardia World a cardio app or a game?

Both. Cardia World is a fitness motivation app and world-building game. Your daily cardio helps a 3D world grow, giving you visible progress to come back to.

Can walking help build my world?

Yes. Walking, running, cycling, and other cardio can contribute to your progress. Record a qualifying cardio session manually or sync supported activity with Apple Health on a compatible iPhone.

Does Cardia World work with Apple Health?

Yes. With your permission on a compatible iPhone, supported cardio workouts saved in Apple Health can sync automatically and contribute build points. Open Cardia World after your workout to refresh your progress. Steps support world vitality; they are not converted into cardio build points. Manual cardio logging is always available.

Is Cardia World free to download?

Yes. Core world building and Friends are free on iPhone and iPad. Optional Cardia World Pro subscriptions add premium world styles, customization, and Cardio Battles.

Private by design

Your progress belongs to you.

Cardia World requires an account to save and restore your world. Activity and world progress are stored on your device and in a private account backup; friends see only the game details you choose to share. There are no ads or cross-app tracking.
