Privacy Policy

Effective September 21, 2026 · Version 1.0

Cardia World is a cardio-motivation and world-building game. Version 1 is designed to work without an account and to keep your information on your device.

Information stored on your device

Cardia World stores the information needed to operate the app locally on your device, including:

  • Your selected daily cardio goal and architecture style
  • Cardio entries you manually record, including date and duration
  • Streaks, construction progress, population, and unlocked milestones
  • Names you choose for settlements and regions
  • App preferences such as sound and haptic settings

This information is used only to operate the app and display your progress. Version 1 does not transmit this information to Cardia World servers or store it remotely.

Information we do not collect

Version 1 does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It does not use:

  • User accounts or cloud profiles
  • Advertising or cross-app tracking
  • Third-party analytics
  • Precise location data
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data
  • Apple Health or HealthKit data

Sharing

If you choose to create or share a world progress card, sharing is initiated by you through the standard sharing options on your device. Cardia World does not automatically upload or publish the card.

Deleting your data

You may delete the information stored by the app by opening Settings → Erase all local data. Removing the app from your device also removes its locally stored data, subject to your device’s backup settings.

Children’s privacy

Cardia World does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users because Version 1 does not collect personal information.

Health disclaimer

Cardia World is a motivational game and is not a medical device. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, monitoring, or treatment. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before beginning or changing an exercise routine when appropriate.

Future changes

If a future version introduces accounts, cloud storage, Apple Health integration, analytics, or another feature that changes data handling, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before that feature is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@cardiaworld.com.

Policy updates

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app or applicable requirements. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current version.
