Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

Cardia World is a fitness motivation game that requires an account. Manual cardio remains available if you decline Apple Health access.

Apple Health and activity

With your permission, Cardia World uses Apple Health workouts and steps, and may count steps through Motion & Fitness on your iPhone. You can also log cardio manually. Activity updates your world, goals, and features you choose to use, such as Cardio Battles.

We store daily step and cardio totals, workout dates, start and end times, durations, and activity labels on your device and in a private account backup. Original Apple Health workout identifiers stay on the device. Service providers that host and process activity include Supabase. We do not write to Apple Health, sell health data, or use it for advertising.

Depending on your choices, friends or people in a Battle or mission can see activity-based game stats, such as scores, cardio minutes, or active-day counts. They do not receive your underlying workout records. Manage access in the app’s social privacy settings and manage Health and Motion & Fitness permissions in iPhone Settings. See our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy for details and rights requests.

Accounts and social features

An account uses your email address for authentication. We store your username, avatar, friendships, requests, messages, profile and billboard photos or videos, privacy choices, reports, Battle invitations and results, and world information as needed for those features. Use a username that does not identify you. Who can visit your world or view your media depends on your social privacy settings. Users can report or block others. Automated safety checks may send text or images you post or share to a moderation provider.

When you are visible on the global leaderboard, your username, mayor name, rank, and game stats, such as buildings and residents, can appear in the app and on the public Cardia World website. Use the visibility control in Global leaderboard to hide your rank. Sharing a snapshot or link through a messaging or social app sends it to the destination you select.

World journal, videos and optional backup

The world journal keeps the latest 36 captured moments for the signed-in account. Journal images remain on the device unless you enable optional private journal backup. Journal backup is separate from the account’s world-progress backup.

Cardia World 1.1.29 can also save a captured world’s rotating-view data and cardio-minute total to create a short video. Optional journal backup is off by default. Turning it on uploads up to 36 recent journal captures and their saved view information to private account storage for restoration on another device. You can turn backup off and delete the cloud journal from World activities. Deleting the account also removes its cloud journal media.

The app also provides individual and bulk deletion and a 30-day journal age limit, checked when the app opens or returns to the foreground. Existing entries receive a fresh 30-day age window when the update first opens. The maximum of 36 recent entries still applies, so adding another can replace the oldest earlier. Expiry deadlines and deletion records are included in optional private backup, and cloud cleanup follows synchronization when online.

Images and videos leave the app when you choose a sharing destination or save them to Photos. Saving journal images directly to Photos requests permission to add images only; this feature does not read your photo library. Videos are saved when you choose Save Video. Copies already saved to Photos are not removed by journal cleanup. Creating a video can earn an in-app reward without posting to social media.

A mayor name you choose is stored as game-world metadata and included in the account’s private world-progress backup. It does not need to be a real person’s name.

Invitations, community activity and widgets

Friend and Battle invitation links use a code or identifier to carry an invitation into the app. We use invitation and acceptance status and, for friendship rewards, qualifying game-day counts. Anyone you forward a link to may be able to open it. Community builds show combined in-game contributions. Cardia World may send account and game messages through its email or notification providers. Recurring progress emails, where available, include a way to unsubscribe.

If you enable the Home Screen widget, it uses an on-device copy of a saved world image, game totals and next-reward information. Widget data is cleared when changing accounts. A widget does not send Health records to other users.

Feedback you review before sending

Settings → Send feedback prepares an email for you to review. Device details and a world image are optional attachments that begin switched off. They are sent only when you choose to include them and send the message.

Purchases

Apple processes Pro subscriptions. The app checks entitlements through StoreKit, and a server verifies signed transaction details for competitive features. Cardia World does not collect payment card numbers.

App usage and optional diagnostics

Cardia World may log account-linked events such as opening the app or using a feature to understand usage and improve the game. These events are separate from workout records.

In versions with usage diagnostics, you can choose to send PostHog a masked session showing app navigation for up to 10 minutes. Text and images are masked and sensitive screens are excluded. The session ends when you stop it, leave the app, switch accounts, or reach the time limit.

Security and deletion

Authentication uses Supabase. The iPhone stores its session in the device Keychain. Backend access rules limit social data according to feature permissions. Use Delete my account in Friends or Settings → Privacy where available to delete your account and associated records. Copies saved separately in Photos, shared elsewhere, or retained by a recipient may require separate removal. Manage App Store subscription renewal separately in Apple Subscriptions.

We retain account and activity data as needed to provide and protect your account and meet legal obligations. Some records can remain in temporary backups or as needed to resolve fraud, safety, or legal matters. Contact support@cardiaworld.com to request access, correction, or deletion. We may confirm account ownership before disclosing data.

Age eligibility

Cardia World is designed for people aged 13 and older. Where the app asks for a birth date, it uses the date on the device to check eligibility and does not send the date to Cardia World. A parent or guardian who believes a child under 13 shared information can email support@cardiaworld.com to report it and request deletion. Include the username or account email if known and keep passwords, identity documents, and health information out of email.

Website analytics

Our public website uses optional Google Analytics to measure visits, traffic sources, pages viewed, and clicks on App Store download buttons. Analytics starts only after you choose Allow analytics. Google may then use analytics cookies and receive basic browser and device information and approximate location. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled.

We remove query strings and fragments from the page addresses sent to Analytics and send only the referring website’s origin. Recognized social campaign labels identify sources such as TikTok and Reddit. We do not send app account details, Apple Health records, passwords, recovery links, invitation codes, or form entries. Account, password-recovery, and invitation pages are excluded. Website button clicks are not confirmed app installs.

Choose Analytics choices in the website footer to allow or decline analytics. Your choice is remembered on this browser for up to 180 days. Declining stops analytics and clears its accessible cookies. We also respect browser Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track requests. You can use the website without allowing analytics. For Google’s handling of analytics data, see Google’s Privacy Policy.

Contact and updates

Privacy questions: support@cardiaworld.com. The effective date identifies revisions.
